---
title: External monitor or second display not detected
---
## Nothing on the display
Check that the display is powered on and set to the correct input. Reseat the cable at both ends, or try another cable or port. If you use a docking station, disconnect it, wait ten seconds and connect it again.

## Windows does not see it
Press Windows key + P and choose Extend or Duplicate. Open Display settings and select Detect. Restart the computer with the display connected so the graphics driver loads it.

## Wrong size or blurry picture
In Display settings pick the recommended resolution and refresh rate. A blurry picture is usually caused by a resolution that does not match the display.
