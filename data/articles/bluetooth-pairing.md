---
title: Pairing Bluetooth headphones and other devices
---
## Pairing a device
Turn the device on and put it in pairing mode, normally by holding its power or Bluetooth button until a light blinks. Open Settings, then Bluetooth and devices, choose Add device and select it from the list.

## Device not listed
Make sure Bluetooth is switched on in the quick settings and that airplane mode is off. Remove the old pairing for that device and pair again. A headset connected to your phone will not appear until you disconnect it there.

## No Bluetooth option on the laptop
Restart the laptop. Open Device Manager and look for a Bluetooth adapter. If none appears, install the latest driver from the service desk software portal or contact them for a hardware check.
