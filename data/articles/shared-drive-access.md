---
title: Getting access to a shared drive or folder
---
## Requesting access
Open the access request form, choose the shared drive or folder and select read only or edit permission. Your manager and the folder owner both need to approve. After approval, sign out and sign in again so the new permissions take effect.

## Access denied errors
If you used to have access and now see Access Denied, your permissions may have expired after a role change. Check the original approval date in your access history and request it again. Do not ask a colleague to share their credentials.

## Mapped drive disappeared
Reconnect to the VPN if you are remote, then open File Explorer and map the network drive again using the path given by your team. Restarting the computer also refreshes mapped drives.
