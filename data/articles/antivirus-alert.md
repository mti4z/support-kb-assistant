---
title: Antivirus warning or detected threat
---
## What to do right away
Do not ignore the message. If the antivirus says it quarantined or removed the file, note the file name and the threat name, then keep working only if the computer behaves normally. Do not open the file again.

## When to disconnect
If the warning repeats, programs open by themselves or the computer is very slow, disconnect from the network and call the service desk. Do not restart until they say so if you saw a ransom message.

## Report it
Open a ticket with a screenshot of the alert. Run a full scan only when the service desk asks you to. If you got the file from an email, also use the phishing report button.
