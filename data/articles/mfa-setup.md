---
title: Setting up multi-factor authentication (MFA)
---
## Enrolling a new device
Install the company approved authenticator app on your phone. Sign in at the security portal, choose Add sign-in method and scan the QR code with the app. Enter the six digit code to finish. Register a backup method, such as a phone number, in case you lose the device.

## Getting a new phone
Before wiping the old phone, move your authenticator accounts using the app's transfer or backup feature. If the old phone is already gone, contact the service desk to clear your registered methods and enroll again.

## Codes are rejected
Authenticator codes depend on the phone clock. Turn on automatic date and time in your phone settings, then try the next code. Wait for a fresh code instead of reusing an old one.
