---
title: Reporting suspicious or phishing emails
---
## How to spot phishing
Be careful with messages that create urgency, ask for passwords or gift cards, come from an unexpected sender or contain links whose address does not match the claimed company. Hover over a link to see the real destination before clicking.

## Report it
Use the Report Phishing button in your email toolbar. This sends a copy to the security team and removes the message from your inbox. Do not forward the suspicious email to colleagues.

## If you already clicked
Disconnect from the network if you entered credentials or opened an attachment, then call the service desk immediately. Change your password from a different clean device. Quick reporting limits the damage, and nobody is punished for reporting a mistake.
