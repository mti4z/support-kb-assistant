---
title: IT checklist for new employees
---
## Before your first day
Your manager submits an onboarding request at least five business days before your start date. IT then prepares your laptop, creates your account and assigns your email address and licenses. You will receive a welcome message with temporary sign-in details.

## First day setup
Collect your laptop from the service desk. Sign in with the temporary password and change it immediately. Enroll in multi-factor authentication and install the standard software bundle from the self-service portal. Confirm you can open email, chat and the intranet.

## Accessing team resources
Access to shared folders, project tools and mailing lists is granted by your manager through an access request. Approval usually takes one business day.
