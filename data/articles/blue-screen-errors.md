---
title: Blue screen of death and unexpected restarts
---
## What to do right away
Write down the stop code shown on the blue screen, for example MEMORY_MANAGEMENT or CRITICAL_PROCESS_DIED, or take a photo of it. Let the computer restart on its own. Save your work often until the problem is solved.

## Common causes
Recent driver or Windows updates, failing memory and overheating are the most common causes. Disconnect newly attached external devices and check that vents are not blocked. Run Windows Memory Diagnostic from the Start menu to test the memory.

## When to open a ticket
If the blue screen happens more than once in a week, open a ticket with the stop code, the time it happened and what you were doing. IT can read the crash dump to find the faulty driver.
