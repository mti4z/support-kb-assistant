---
title: Requesting new software
---
## Approved software catalog
Check the self-service portal first. Most common applications, such as browsers, PDF readers, office tools and development editors, can be installed in a few clicks without approval.

## Requesting something not in the catalog
Open a ticket with the software name, the version, the business reason and the license cost if known. Security reviews every request for vendor risk and data handling. Standard reviews take five business days. Free tools that store company data in the cloud need the longest review.

## Licenses and renewals
Paid licenses are tied to your cost center. When you no longer need a license, return it through the portal so another person can use it. Unused licenses are reclaimed after 90 days of inactivity.
