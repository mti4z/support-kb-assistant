---
title: Windows updates stuck or failing
---
## Update seems frozen
Updates can take a long time on a slow connection. Wait at least two hours before acting and keep the computer plugged in. If the percentage has not changed after that, restart the computer.

## Updates keep failing
Open Settings, Windows Update and run the troubleshooter. Make sure the disk has at least 15 GB of free space. Restart and try again, since many updates install only after a reboot.

## Error codes
Write down the error code and contact the service desk. Do not interrupt the computer by holding the power button while it says it is installing updates unless the service desk tells you to.
