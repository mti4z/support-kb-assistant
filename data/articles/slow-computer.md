---
title: Computer running slowly
---
## Restart first
Many performance problems disappear after a full restart. Choose Restart instead of Shut down, because Shut down on Windows may keep parts of the previous session. Computers that stay on for weeks accumulate updates waiting to install.

## Find what is using resources
Open Task Manager with Ctrl+Shift+Esc and sort the Processes tab by CPU, Memory and Disk. Close browsers tabs and apps you are not using. If one process always uses most of the memory, note its name and include it in your ticket.

## Free disk space
When the disk is almost full, the computer becomes slow. Empty the recycle bin, remove old downloads and run Disk Cleanup. Keep at least 15 percent of the disk free. Move large files to the shared drive or cloud storage.
