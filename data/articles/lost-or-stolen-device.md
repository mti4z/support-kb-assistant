---
title: Lost or stolen laptop or phone
---
## Act immediately
Report the loss to the service desk as soon as you notice, even outside office hours, using the emergency security line. Give the device type, the last place you had it and whether it was locked.

## What IT will do
The security team will remotely lock or wipe the device, revoke your sessions and reset your credentials. Disk encryption protects data on laptops, but a quick report is still required for compliance.

## Replacement
After the incident report is filed, your manager approves a replacement. For theft, also file a police report and attach the reference number to the ticket.
