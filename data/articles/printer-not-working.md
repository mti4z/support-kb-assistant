---
title: Printer is offline or jobs are stuck
---
## Printer shows as offline
Check that the printer is powered on and connected to the network, with no error message on its display. On your computer open Settings, Printers and scanners, select the printer and make sure "Use printer offline" is not enabled. Remove the printer and add it again from the company print server.

## Jobs stuck in the queue
Open the printer queue and cancel all documents. If a job refuses to disappear, restart the Print Spooler service from the Services app and try again. Large files with many images are a frequent cause of jams in the queue, so try printing a smaller test page first.

## Paper jams and toner
For a paper jam, switch the printer off and follow the diagram on the display to remove the paper gently. For low toner or ink, do not open the cartridge. Request a replacement through the service desk.
