---
title: Scanning documents to email
---
## Scan to your own mailbox
At the office multifunction device, sign in with your badge, choose Scan to email and select your own address. Place the paper in the feeder, pick PDF and press start. The file arrives in your inbox within a few minutes.

## Scan does not arrive
Check that you entered your address correctly and look in the Junk folder. Scans bigger than 25 MB are rejected, so lower the resolution to 200 dpi or scan fewer pages at a time.

## Scan to folder
If scan to folder fails, the shared folder may no longer be mapped to your account. Ask the service desk to check your permissions.
