---
title: Leaving the company and returning equipment
---
## Before your last day
Your manager will give you an offboarding checklist. Hand over files and shared mailboxes to a colleague and move any personal files off your work computer, because accounts are closed on your last day.

## Equipment to return
Return the laptop, charger, docking station, headset, badge and any company phone to the service desk or send it with the prepaid courier box. Sign the equipment return form so the items are removed from your name.

## Accounts and access
Access to email, VPN and applications is disabled at the end of your last working day. Remove the company email and authenticator accounts from your personal phone.
