---
title: Calendar events missing or not syncing
---
## Invitations not arriving
Check the Junk folder and the rules in your mailbox, because a rule can move invitations out of the inbox. Ask the organizer to send the invitation again. Make sure your mailbox is not full.

## Different devices show different events
Open the calendar app on your phone and force a manual sync. If it does not help, remove the work account from the phone and add it again. Events you created on the computer appear on the phone only after the next sync.

## Wrong times
Check that the time zone is the same on all devices. Recurring meetings created in another time zone can shift after daylight saving changes.
