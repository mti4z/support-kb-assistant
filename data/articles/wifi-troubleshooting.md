---
title: Wi-Fi keeps dropping or will not connect
---
## Quick checks
Turn Wi-Fi off and on again, then forget the network and join it again with the password. Move closer to the router or access point and away from microwaves or thick walls. Check whether other devices on the same network also lose the signal.

## Restart the network equipment
In the office, report the problem to the service desk rather than restarting shared equipment. At home, unplug the router for 30 seconds, plug it back in and wait two minutes until all lights are stable.

## Update the adapter driver
An outdated wireless driver is a common cause of random disconnections on laptops. Open Device Manager, expand Network adapters, right click the wireless adapter and choose Update driver. Restart afterwards.
