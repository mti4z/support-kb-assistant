---
title: Connecting to your office computer remotely
---
## Before you start
Connect to the VPN first. Remote Desktop only works from outside the office when the VPN is active. Leave your office computer turned on and locked, not shut down.

## Connecting
Open the Remote Desktop Connection app, enter your office computer name and sign in with your usual username and password. If the name is not found, use the IP address the service desk gave you.

## Problems
A black screen or a message that the computer cannot be reached usually means it is off or asleep. Ask a colleague to wake it, or request that the service desk enable Wake on LAN. The connection is slow on poor home internet, so lower the display quality in the options.
