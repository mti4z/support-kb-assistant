---
title: Screen sharing problems in meetings
---
## Share button greyed out
The meeting organizer can restrict who is allowed to present. Ask them to make you a presenter. Join the meeting from the desktop app, since the browser version has more limits.

## Black rectangle or frozen picture
Some applications, such as protected video players, show as black when shared. Share a single window instead of the entire display, or turn off hardware acceleration in the meeting app settings. Update the graphics driver if it keeps happening.

## Others cannot see your share
Close extra windows and stop other applications that use the camera or screen capture. Leave the meeting and rejoin. On a Mac the app needs the screen recording permission in system privacy settings.
