---
title: Keyboard or mouse not responding
---
## Wireless devices
Replace or recharge the batteries and switch the device off and on. Reconnect the USB receiver in a different port, or pair the device again if it uses Bluetooth. Keep the receiver away from metal surfaces and docking stations.

## Wired devices
Try another USB port and check the cable for damage. Test the device on another computer to find out whether the device or the computer is at fault.

## Wrong characters or stuck keys
Wrong characters usually mean the keyboard layout or language is incorrect. Open the language settings and remove layouts you do not use. If a key sticks, clean around it with compressed air and request a replacement from the service desk if it continues.

## Cursor jumps or lags
Clean the sensor and use the mouse on a flat, non shiny surface. Check that the touchpad is not being touched by your palm while typing.
