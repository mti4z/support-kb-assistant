---
title: Resetting your company password
---
## Self-service reset
Go to the sign-in page and choose "Forgot password". Enter your work email and approve the verification code sent to your registered phone or authenticator app. Pick a new password that is at least 14 characters long and different from your last five passwords. The change applies to email, VPN and the intranet within about five minutes.

## Account locked after failed attempts
Five wrong passwords in a row lock the account for 30 minutes. Wait for the lock to expire and try again, or ask the service desk to unlock it immediately after verifying your identity. Do not keep retrying, because each failed attempt restarts the timer.

## If you cannot receive the verification code
If you changed phone numbers or lost your authenticator, open a ticket from a colleague's device or call the service desk. A technician will verify your identity with your manager before resetting your second factor.
