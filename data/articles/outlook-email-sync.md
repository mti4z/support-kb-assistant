---
title: Outlook not sending or receiving email
---
## Check connection status
Look at the bottom bar of Outlook. If it says Working Offline or Disconnected, open the Send/Receive tab and turn off Work Offline. Confirm you have internet access and, when away from the office, that the VPN is connected if your role requires it.

## Mailbox full
A mailbox that reached its size limit stops receiving new messages. Empty Deleted Items, archive old email and remove large attachments. The default mailbox limit is 50 GB and you get a warning at 90 percent.

## Rebuild the profile
If Outlook crashes at startup or never syncs, create a new Outlook profile from Control Panel, Mail, Show Profiles, Add. Sign in with your work account and let it download the mailbox again. This can take an hour for large mailboxes.
