---
title: No sound or microphone in video calls
---
## Check devices in the meeting app
In the call settings select the correct speaker and microphone from the device list. Run the built-in test to see the level bar move. Make sure the microphone is not muted, either in the app or with the physical key on your headset.

## Operating system permissions
Open the privacy settings and confirm that the meeting app is allowed to use the microphone and camera. After changing permissions, completely close and reopen the app.

## Headset problems
Unplug and reconnect the headset or re-pair the Bluetooth device. Try a different USB port. If a Bluetooth headset sounds poor, switch the output to its stereo profile instead of the hands-free profile when not speaking.
