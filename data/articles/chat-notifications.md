---
title: Chat notifications missing or delayed
---
## No sound or popup
Open the chat app settings, then Notifications, and confirm that banners and sound are on. Check that Focus assist or Do not disturb is off on your computer. Your status set to Busy can silence alerts.

## Late messages
Messages that arrive late are often caused by an unstable connection or a sleeping app. Sign out of the chat app and sign in again, then clear its cache from the app menu.

## On the phone
Allow notifications for the chat app in the phone settings and disable battery saving for it, which would otherwise suspend it in the background.
