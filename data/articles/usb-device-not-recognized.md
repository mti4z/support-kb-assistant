---
title: USB drive or external disk not recognized
---
## First checks
Try a different USB port, preferably directly on the computer instead of a hub. Test the drive on another computer. A drive that does not light up or spin may need its own power adapter.

## Drive connected but not visible
Open Disk Management and look for the drive. If it has no drive letter, assign one. A drive marked as unallocated or raw must not be formatted before you ask the service desk, because formatting erases the data.

## Restricted devices
Company policy blocks unknown storage devices on some computers. If you get a message that the device is blocked, request approval through the service desk instead of trying to work around it.
