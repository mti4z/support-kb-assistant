---
title: Browser running slowly or showing outdated pages
---
## Clear cached data
Press Ctrl + Shift + Delete, choose cached images and files and clear them. A hard refresh with Ctrl + F5 forces the page to load the newest version. Clear cookies only if the site still misbehaves, because you will have to sign in again.

## Slow browsing
Close tabs you do not need and disable extensions one by one to find the one causing trouble. Check the task manager of the browser to see which tab uses the most memory. Restart the browser at least once a week.

## Site loads in another browser
If a site works in a different browser, update your browser to the latest version and reset its settings. Report sites that fail everywhere to the service desk with the address and the error message.
