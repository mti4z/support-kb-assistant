---
title: Setting up company email on a phone
---
## Adding the account
Install the company approved mail app from the app store and sign in with your work address. Approve the sign in with your authenticator app. The phone may ask to install a management profile, which keeps company data separate from personal data.

## Requirements
The phone needs a screen lock and an up to date operating system. Phones that are rooted or have a jailbreak are blocked.

## Mail does not arrive
Check the sync settings of the account and make sure the phone has internet access. Remove the account and add it again if needed. If you change your password, sign in again in the app.
